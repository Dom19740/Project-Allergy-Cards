Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 2026)

This Cookie Policy explains how Simple Allergy Alert (operated by Dominic Buzugbe, trading as Simple Allergy Alert) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit simpleallergyalert.com or use the web app at app.simpleallergyalert.com.

For more information about how we handle your personal data more broadly, please see our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve performance, and provide information to website owners. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage — we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

2. What Cookies Do We Use?

We use the following categories of cookies:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website and web app to function. They cannot be switched off. They are typically set in response to actions you take, such as completing a purchase or setting your preferences.

We may also store a small referral attribution value in your browser's local storage so that affiliate links can continue to work across visits and page navigation. This is used for attribution and does not require a separate permission prompt.

Cookie / Technology Provider Purpose
Session cookies Vercel (hosting) Maintain session state and ensure the web app functions correctly
Payment session cookies Lemon Squeezy Required to process your premium upgrade purchase and maintain checkout session state

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website and app. The information is aggregated and anonymous. We use this to improve our services.

Cookie / Technology Provider Purpose
Firebase Analytics Google Collects anonymised usage data including app events, session duration, and device information to help us understand and improve the app. Data is not used for advertising.
Google Analytics Google Tracks and analyses use of the website. To opt out, visit https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we use.

Cookie / Technology Provider Purpose
Firebase Crashlytics Google Collects crash reports and device information on the Android app to help us identify and fix stability issues. Not used for advertising.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. The relevant third-party privacy policies are:

  • Google Privacy Policy (covers Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics, Google Translate API)
  • Firebase Privacy Information
  • Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. The method for doing so varies by browser. Please refer to your browser's help documentation.
  • Google Analytics opt-out — Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google's ad settings — Manage how Google uses your data at adssettings.google.com.

Please note that if you choose to block or delete cookies, some features of the website or web app may not function correctly — in particular, the payment flow for the premium upgrade relies on session cookies set by Lemon Squeezy.

5. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website or web app, you will be asked to consent to non-essential cookies (analytics and functional cookies). Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent as they are essential for the service to function.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your cookies in your browser settings and revisiting the site, or by contacting us at info@simpleallergyalert.com.
